About CORPSTITCH

A professional manufacturing partner for uniforms, formalwear & custom garments

CORPSTITCH is a Bengaluru-based uniform and garment manufacturing partner serving businesses, institutions and organisations. We work across corporate, industrial, educational and hospitality requirements, helping customers source garments that reflect their brand, suit their working environment and meet day-to-day needs.

Our range brings together customised uniforms, corporate formalwear, blazers, suits, traditional occasion wear and made-to-requirement garments. Fabric, garment details, quantities and finishing can be discussed with our team before a quotation is prepared.

Alongside manufacturing, we offer wholesale and retail fabric supply, tailoring and alterations, and B2B supply support. We welcome enquiries from companies, schools, colleges, hotels, retailers and wholesalers, across India (including South India) and for international and Gulf-region requirements.

Every order starts with understanding the requirement: who the garments are for, the preferred style, quantity, fabric and customisation details. We then discuss suitable options and next steps.

A few things worth knowing

Frequently asked questions.

Can I enquire about customised garments?

Yes. Describe the garment and share reference images, colours, logo details or specifications. Customisation options and feasibility are discussed for your requirement.

Do you handle bulk and business enquiries?

Yes, we welcome bulk and B2B enquiries from businesses, teams and educational institutions. Include your estimated quantity and garment details.

How is a quotation prepared?

Pricing depends on garment type, quantity, fabric, construction and customisation. Share as much as you can so the quotation reflects your actual request.

What if I do not have a final design?

Start with a description, inspiration images or an existing garment. Explain what you like and which details are flexible.

Can one enquiry include different garments or sizes?

Yes. List the items with an approximate quantity for each, plus any size or department breakdown.

Can I request a particular delivery date?

Share your timeline at the start. Production and delivery depend on the order specifications, so timing is confirmed for each enquiry.

What should I do before confirming an order?

Review the garment description, quantities, sizes, fabric, branding, quotation, payment terms and timeline. Ask about anything unclear first.
